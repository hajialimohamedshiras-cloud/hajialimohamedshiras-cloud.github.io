The Story & Resume

My Journey

My career started with a passion for visual communication — the precision of CMYK colour, the discipline of print pre-press, and the craft of creating designs that speak across language barriers. Working in fast-paced environments like Mobile Art & Print Shop and Nethra Printers taught me to deliver under pressure, meet tight deadlines, and collaborate within high-performance teams.

But curiosity doesn't stay still. The more I designed for digital platforms, the more I became fascinated by the infrastructure beneath the surface — how data moves, how networks are secured, how systems are engineered to scale. That curiosity led me to enrol in the HND in Network Engineering at ESOFT University, where I'm now building the technical half of my professional identity.

The result? A professional who can design the front-end and understand the back-end. A creative who thinks like an engineer — and an engineer with a designer's eye.
